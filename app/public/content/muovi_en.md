# Plastic waste

Plastic recycling is an important part of waste management, as it enables the reuse of valuable raw materials and reduces the need for fossil-based raw materials. In Finland, plastic packaging waste is collected especially from households through separate collection systems, and collection points can be found throughout the country.

**Sorting instructions:**

Always follow regional and collection-point-specific instructions. The sorting instructions presented here are general in nature and reflect current common practice.

**YES:**

- plastic food packaging (for example yogurt containers, ketchup bottles)
- detergent and cosmetic packaging
- plastic bags and wrappers

**NO:**

- dirty plastic packaging → mixed waste
- items containing PVC plastic, such as tarpaulins or rainwear
- other plastics that are not packaging (for example toys)

Plastic packaging can often be returned to collection points or stores using return systems. From the collection points, plastic waste is transported to processing facilities, where it is sorted, cleaned, and recycled for the manufacture of new products.

**Processing and utilization:**

Collected plastic packaging is sorted and processed for reuse. Recycled plastic is used to make, for example, new packaging, plastic pipes, or composite products. Plastics that cannot be recycled can be used for energy recovery in waste-to-energy plants. Recycling saves natural resources and reduces the amount of waste.

**Finding a recycling point:**

If you have plastic packaging to recycle, find the nearest recycling point in the ReCycler service by selecting the category **“Plastic.”** The service shows collection points in your area that are intended especially for household waste.
