# Glass

Glass packaging is recyclable and provides raw material for making new products. Recycle glass bottles and jars properly so that valuable materials can be recovered. Collection points for glass packaging can be found in the ReCycler service.

**Sorting instructions:**

Always follow regional instructions and the practices of recycling points.

**YES:**

- glass bottles
- glass jars

**NO:**

- drinking glasses, oven dishes
- lamps and light fixtures
- window glass, mirror glass
- porcelain, ceramics

Remove caps and lids. Deposit bottles should be returned to stores. Glass packaging is transported to recycling facilities, where it is crushed and sorted. Find the nearest recycling point in the ReCycler service by selecting **“Glass packaging.”**
