# Styrofoam

Styrofoam, or polystyrene, may belong in plastic packaging waste, mixed waste, or construction waste depending on its use and condition. Always follow local and collection-point-specific instructions. The guidance below is general in nature and reflects current common practice.

**Sorting instructions:**

**YES (Plastic packaging waste)**

- styrofoam packaging, such as:
  - empty and clean food-grade styrofoam (for example meat trays, cultured milk or yogurt containers made of styrofoam)
  - protective pieces from electrical devices and household appliances (if they are part of the packaging)
  - styrofoam balls and pellets (for example as filling in online shopping deliveries), sealed in a plastic bag so they do not spread into the environment
- large styrofoam pieces are recommended to be broken into smaller parts before sorting

**YES (Mixed waste)**

- styrofoam that is not packaging (for example coolers, craft styrofoam)
- dirty or moisture-exposed styrofoam (for example fish boxes)
- styrofoam that crumbles heavily and does not stay intact

**YES (Construction waste / waste station)**

- styrofoam originating from construction and insulation work sites (for example EPS boards)
- styrofoam with paint, glue, asphalt coating, or other construction materials
- large quantities of styrofoam → take to a recycling station or similar waste station (may be subject to a fee)

**NO (Not energy waste)**

- styrofoam is generally not classified separately as energy waste
- it is sorted case by case as plastic packaging, mixed waste, or construction waste

**Collection and transport:**

- styrofoam accepted as plastic packaging is taken to plastic packaging collection
- break large pieces into smaller ones or take them directly to a waste station
- because styrofoam spreads easily, packing it in a sealed plastic bag is recommended
- construction styrofoam does not belong in household waste collection → it must be taken to a waste station

**Color code:**

- plastic packaging waste: yellow
- mixed waste: dark or black
- construction waste: no uniform color code

**Processing:**

- styrofoam is generally not suitable for mechanical recycling, but it can be incinerated for energy
- from plastic packaging waste, suitable fractions are separated for recycling and energy recovery
- at waste stations, large quantities can be handled separately

**Utilization:**

- styrofoam can be used for energy recovery through incineration, producing district heat and electricity
- styrofoam balls delivered in a sealed bag help prevent littering and enable safer handling
