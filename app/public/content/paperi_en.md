# Paper

Recycling paper is an essential part of conserving natural resources. Recycled paper can be reused, for example, in the production of newsprint, tissue paper, and packaging materials. Take used paper to the proper collection points so that it can be utilized efficiently.

The recycling points shown in the ReCycler service are intended especially for household waste.

**Sorting instructions:**

Always follow regional and collection-point-specific instructions. The following sorting instructions are general in nature and reflect current common practice.

**YES:**

- newspapers and magazines
- advertising mail and brochures
- envelopes (including window envelopes)
- note paper and printouts
- notebooks and the paper parts of books (with covers removed)

**NO:**

- wet or dirty paper → mixed waste
- carton/cardboard packaging → carton recycling
- aluminum-coated or plastic-coated paper → mixed waste
- corrugated cardboard → cardboard collection

Paper waste should be kept dry and clean before recycling. From the collection points, paper is transported to processing facilities, where it is sorted, cleaned, and processed into new products.

**Processing and recycling:**

In the paper recycling process, the material is turned into pulp, which can be used, for example, in the manufacture of newspapers, tissue paper, or packaging materials. Recycling saves wood-based raw material and reduces the amount of waste.

**Finding a recycling point:**

If you have paper waste to recycle, find the nearest recycling point in the ReCycler service by selecting the category **“Paper”** and checking the collection points available in your area.
