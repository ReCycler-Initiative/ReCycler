# Electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE)

Electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE) contains materials such as metals and plastics that can be recycled into raw materials for new products. It is important to return discarded electrical devices to proper recycling points so that environmentally harmful substances are handled correctly and valuable materials can be recovered. Sorting stations that accept electrical equipment can be found in the ReCycler service.

**Sorting instructions:**

Follow regional instructions and the practices of sorting stations.

**YES:**

- household appliances such as refrigerators, stoves, and washing machines
- consumer electronics such as televisions, stereos, and game consoles
- small electrical appliances such as hand mixers, coffee makers, and hair dryers
- computers, tablets, mobile phones, and their accessories
- lighting fixtures, except incandescent and halogen bulbs
- power tools such as drills and saws
- batteries and rechargeable batteries (if no separate collection is available)

**NO:**

- incandescent and halogen bulbs (mixed waste)
- ordinary household waste
- hazardous waste such as oils and chemicals
- electrical equipment permanently installed in buildings (for example electric radiators and air-source heat pumps, which belong in construction waste)

Electrical devices should be returned intact whenever possible so that they can be dismantled and recycled safely. Remove any batteries and rechargeable batteries before returning the device, unless they are integrated into it.

**Collection and recycling:**  
WEEE waste is transported to recycling facilities, where the devices are dismantled into parts. In recycling, metals, plastics, and other usable materials are separated and directed to reuse. Environmentally harmful substances, such as mercury and lead, are handled using special methods.

**Additional information:**  
Recycling WEEE helps conserve natural resources and reduce greenhouse gas emissions. By recycling electrical devices properly, you help ensure that harmful substances do not end up in the environment. Find the nearest WEEE collection point in the ReCycler service.
