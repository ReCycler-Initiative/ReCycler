# End-of-life textiles

End-of-life textiles are broken, worn-out, or otherwise unusable textiles that can no longer be reused. Recycling end-of-life textiles is important so that valuable materials can be recovered for new products, such as recycled fiber, insulation, or new textiles. Sorting stations that accept end-of-life textiles can be found in the ReCycler service.

**Sorting instructions:**

Always follow regional instructions and the practices of sorting stations.

**YES:**

- broken and worn-out clothing
- unusable household textiles, such as curtains, towels, and bed sheets
- clean and dry textiles without hard parts such as buttons or zippers
- jeans, wool sweaters, and other textile materials suitable for recycling

**NO:**

- wet, moldy, or dirty textiles
- underwear, socks, and tights
- carpets, pillows, blankets, and padded items
- soft toys and other stuffed textile products

**Please note:**  
End-of-life textiles should be packed tightly in a plastic bag so that they stay dry and clean for recycling. Only clean and dry textiles can be used as recycled material.

**Collection and transport:**  
End-of-life textiles are collected regionally at sorting stations or separate collection points. Check collection locations in your area in the ReCycler service or on the website of your local waste management company.

**Processing:**  
End-of-life textiles are sorted and processed mechanically or chemically.
