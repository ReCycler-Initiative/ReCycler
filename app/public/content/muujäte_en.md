# Other waste

Not all waste fits clearly into a specific recycling category. Such waste is often classified as mixed waste or special waste, depending on its characteristics and treatment needs. It is important to follow local sorting instructions and use the correct waste management solutions to minimize environmental impacts.

**Sorting instructions:**

Always follow local instructions and make sure the waste is taken to the correct place for treatment.

**YES:**

- municipal waste that cannot be recycled
- non-hazardous construction waste (for example plasterboard, ceramics)
- contaminated or damaged packaging that cannot be recycled
- textiles that are not suitable for recycling

**NO:**

- hazardous waste → take to a hazardous waste collection point
- recyclable waste such as metal, glass, or paper → take to the appropriate recycling categories
- biowaste → take to biowaste collection
- waste electrical and electronic equipment → take to WEEE collection

Waste that belongs in mixed waste is often incinerated for energy production. The energy generated is then used, for example, for heating. Energy recovery is the last option for waste when recycling is not possible.

**Finding a recycling point:**

If you have waste that does not fit into other recycling categories, you can check the nearest mixed waste reception point in the ReCycler service by selecting **“Mixed waste.”** Also remember to check local instructions for handling special waste such as hazardous waste.
