# Batteries and small rechargeable batteries

Batteries and small rechargeable batteries often contain hazardous substances that can harm the environment if they are not handled properly. Take all used batteries and small rechargeable batteries to the correct collection points. The recovered materials can be recycled into raw materials for new products or used for energy recovery.

The recycling points found, for example, through the Kierrätys.info service are intended especially for household waste.

**Sorting instructions:**

Always follow regional and collection-point-specific instructions. The following sorting instructions are general in nature and reflect current common practice.

**YES:**

- household batteries (for example AA and AAA batteries)
- button batteries
- rechargeable battery packs (for example from mobile phones and laptops)
- rechargeable batteries for power tools
- electric toothbrush batteries
- hearing aid batteries

**NO:**

- vehicle batteries → belong in separate collection
- large industrial batteries
- large lithium batteries and electric vehicle batteries

Batteries can often be returned to the stores where they were purchased or to similar retailers that have collection points for batteries and small rechargeable batteries. From the collection points, batteries are transported to processing facilities, where they are dismantled and recycled safely.

**Processing and recycling:**

Battery recycling recovers metals such as zinc, manganese, and steel. These are used in the manufacture of new products. Most of the lithium can also be recovered and used, among other things, in the manufacture of new lithium batteries. Batteries and rechargeable batteries should always be taken to recycling, because if they end up in nature they can cause serious environmental damage.

**Finding a recycling point:**

If you have batteries or small rechargeable batteries to recycle, find the nearest recycling point in the ReCycler service by selecting the category **“Batteries and small rechargeable batteries”** and checking the collection points available in your area.
