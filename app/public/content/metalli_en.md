# Metal

Metal packaging is recyclable and can be used in the manufacture of new metal products. When recycled properly, metal saves natural resources and reduces the amount of waste. Collection points for metal packaging can be found in the ReCycler service.

**Sorting instructions:**

Always follow regional instructions and the practices of recycling points.

**YES:**

- food cans
- aluminum cans and foil
- metal lids and caps
- aerosol cans (empty)

**NO:**

- electrical devices and batteries
- metal objects such as tools
- residues of hazardous substances

Metal packaging is crushed and sorted at recycling facilities. Find the nearest recycling point in the ReCycler service by selecting **“Metal packaging.”**
