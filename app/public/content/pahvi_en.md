# Cardboard

Recycling cardboard is important, because recycled cardboard can be used to make new products such as packaging materials and core board. Take all used cardboard to the proper collection points so that it can be utilized efficiently.

The recycling points shown in the ReCycler service are intended especially for household waste.

**Sorting instructions:**

Always follow regional and collection-point-specific instructions. The following sorting instructions are general in nature and reflect current common practice.

**YES:**

- cardboard boxes
- brown corrugated cardboard packaging
- corrugated cardboard
- cardboard mailing packages
- clean and dry cardboard without plastic or tape

**NO:**

- dirty cardboard → mixed waste
- plastic-coated or foil-coated cardboard → mixed waste
- wet cardboard → mixed waste

Cardboard can often also be returned to stores or collection points that accept cardboard waste. From the collection points, cardboard is transported to processing facilities, where it is sorted, cleaned, and prepared for recycling.

**Processing and recycling:**

In the cardboard recycling process, it is turned into new raw material that can be used, for example, in the manufacture of new packaging materials. Recycling saves natural resources and reduces the amount of waste while also lowering environmental impacts.

**Finding a recycling point:**

If you have cardboard to recycle, find the nearest recycling point in the ReCycler service by selecting the category **“Cardboard”** and checking the collection points available in your area.
