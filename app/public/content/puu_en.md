# Wood

Recycling wood saves natural resources and reduces the amount of waste ending up in landfills. Recycling can make use of materials such as wood panels, furniture, garden waste, and other wood-based materials.

**Sorting instructions:**

Always follow regional and collection-point-specific instructions. The following sorting instructions are general in nature and reflect current common practice.

**YES:**

- wood panels, furniture, and construction waste
- garden waste (branches, leaves, grass clippings)
- wooden packaging material (for example old boxes and box lids)

**NO:**

- painted or varnished wooden items → mixed waste
- treated or chemically treated wood → mixed waste
- wood and construction materials containing plastic or metal → mixed waste

Wood waste is processed by incineration or other methods through which wood materials or energy can be recovered for reuse.
