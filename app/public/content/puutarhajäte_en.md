# Garden waste

Garden waste is recyclable and can be turned into compost soil and other soil improvement products. Recycle garden waste properly so that natural resources can be used efficiently. Collection points for garden waste can be found in the ReCycler service.

**Sorting instructions:**

Always follow regional instructions and the practices of collection points.

**YES:**

- raking waste
- twigs and branches
- leaves and needles
- grass clippings

**NO:**

- plastic flower pots
- stones and soil material
- biowaste such as food scraps
- hazardous waste such as oils

Garden waste is transported to composting facilities, where it is processed into soil improvement products. Branches and twigs can be chipped and used, for example, in energy production. Find the nearest recycling point in the ReCycler service by selecting **“Garden waste.”**
