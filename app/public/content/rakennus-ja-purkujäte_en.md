# Construction waste

Construction and demolition waste includes many kinds of materials that can be reused or recycled. Sorting waste correctly at the site helps ensure that valuable materials are recovered and handled in an environmentally responsible way. Collection points for construction and demolition waste can be found in the ReCycler service.

**Sorting instructions:**

When sorting construction and demolition waste, the following waste fractions should be taken into account:

- concrete, brick, mineral tile, and ceramic waste
- gypsum-based waste
- untreated wood waste
- metal waste
- glass waste
- plastic waste
- paper and cardboard waste
- soil and stone material waste
- hazardous waste, such as asbestos

**Please note:**

- Hazardous waste and significant amounts of gypsum waste must be separated already at the place where they are generated.
- By sorting different waste fractions, you save costs and ensure that materials can be used as raw materials.

Unsorted construction and demolition waste can be delivered to processing facilities, where materials suitable for recycling and energy recovery can be separated. In facilities with good separation technology, as much as 85–90% of the waste volume can be utilized. Find the nearest recycling point in the ReCycler service by selecting **“Construction and demolition waste.”**
