# Carton

It is important to recycle cardboard packaging, as it provides valuable raw material for making new products. Recycled cardboard can be used, for example, to make paper, core board, or new packaging. Take all used cardboard packaging to the proper collection points.

The recycling points shown in the ReCycler service are intended especially for household waste.

**Sorting instructions:**

Always follow regional and collection-point-specific instructions. The following sorting instructions are general in nature and reflect current common practice.

**YES:**

- cardboard boxes
- milk cartons and juice cartons (including those with screw caps)
- cardboard packaging for dry foods (for example biscuit boxes)
- cardboard packaging for detergent products
- cardboard disposable tableware and cardboard wrappers

**NO:**

- dirty cardboard packaging, take it to mixed waste
- packaging that contains a large amount of plastic
- foil-coated packaging that has not been cleaned

Cardboard packaging can often also be returned to stores or collection points that accept used cardboard. From the collection points, cardboard packaging is transported to processing facilities, where it is sorted, cleaned, and recycled into new products.

**Processing and recycling:**

Cardboard packaging yields recyclable fiber that can be used, for example, to make new packaging. In the recycling process, any impurities are separated from the cardboard, and the processed fiber can be reused. Recycling saves natural resources and reduces the amount of waste.

**Finding a recycling point:**

If you have cardboard waste to recycle, find the nearest recycling point in the ReCycler service by selecting the category **“Cardboard”** and checking the collection points available in your area.
